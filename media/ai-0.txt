AI generation and engineering judgment

Generated code can accelerate visible progress while leaving questions about understanding and rationale unresolved. Distinguish technical, cognitive and intent debt. Review generated implementations through concrete scenarios and failure tests rather than treating a working demonstration as complete proof of quality.

Define the submission promise

Start with the student’s uncertainty: did the submission succeed? Identify stakeholder needs and a trustworthy source of status. The LEARN-inspired architecture is a hypothetical teaching model. Its responsibility boxes explain cooperation without committing to separate services. Include interruptions, concurrency and authorization in the problem before choosing implementation details.
