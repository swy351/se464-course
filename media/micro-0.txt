One deployable application

Distinguish replication from decomposition. Replicas run the same application to serve more requests; microservices divide responsibilities into separately deployable components. A monolith often simplifies development and transactions. Its suitability depends on the system and team, so begin with concrete change and coordination problems rather than assuming more services are automatically better.

Choosing service boundaries

Choose service boundaries around responsibilities and change patterns. Network APIs hide implementation details, but calls can fail independently and cross-service data updates require deliberate recovery. Compare the gain in independent deployment with the added testing and operational burden. A service split is justified when those benefits matter enough to pay the coordination cost.
