Design exercise: feeds, fan-out and locality
Part 2 • Synthetic narration

Partitioning a social graph

Partitioning by follower can keep a person’s follow list and prepared feed together. It cannot simultaneously place every followed author’s original posts beside every reader without duplication. Social relationships cross partition boundaries, so a normalized read may fetch from several owners. The graph model makes the tension visible: balancing vertices and keeping related vertices together are competing objectives.

Hybrid feeds and the celebrity exception

A hybrid design precomputes updates for ordinary authors and merges selected high-fan-out authors’ recent posts when a reader opens the feed. This avoids sending every popular post to millions of inactive followers. The threshold is a workload decision: compare expected fan-out cost against the reads that would need an on-demand merge, including freshness and cache behavior for popular content.
