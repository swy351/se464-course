Quorums and their limits

For one key replicated N times, a write may wait for W acknowledgements and a read for R responses If R+W>N, the selected sets overlap. New writes cannot obtain all three acknowledgements. Reads may still obtain one response, subject to the system’s consistency and failure rules.
