Also vary uncertain ratings. In the original ordering, reducing A’s Search rating from 3 to 2 lowers its total by about 0.4583, easily reversing the narrow lead. This identifies where a prototype or benchmark may be valuable. Sensitivity analysis asks whether the conclusion survives plausible changes, not how to tune the inputs until a favorite wins. The case emphasizes revisiting the framework during planning cycles as business priorities and technologies change. Retain the scenarios, rankings, evidence and rationale so the team can update the decision without reconstructing every discussion. Add new alternatives or scenarios when justified. The ranking changes after stakeholders prioritize recovery. Does that prove the method is broken? No. A preference-sensitive method should respond to a real priority change. 8. Understand technical debt as a quality gap — 8. Understand technical debt as a quality gap

Jeromy extends the same framework to technical debt. A deliberate shortcut can buy delivery time by accepting lower quality in specific scenarios. The time saved is the benefit obtained; the obligation is the gap between the desired and actual quality of the system. Repayment effort need not equal the effort initially saved. New example: a team ships a fixed-format importer in two weeks instead of spending six weeks on a general adapter framework. It gains four weeks now, but the actual system performs poorly on a future “add a source” scenario. Later integrations, changed code and new requirements may make closing that gap more or less expensive than four weeks.

8. Understand technical debt as a quality gap — Reason through the consequences

Compare the actual design and a desired design against the same current scenario tree. Inspect weighted per-scenario gaps to locate the important deficiencies, then estimate the work and risk of addressing them. Do not confuse a quality-score difference with engineer-days; the score helps prioritize investigation, while the effort estimate comes from engineering analysis. The target can change over time. If new-source integration becomes central to the business, its quality gap matters more. If the system is retired, that particular investment may no longer have value. Document the shortcut, affected scenarios, intended benefit, current consequences, and a review trigger. The lecture does not establish a fixed interest-rate formula for technical debt. The team saved four weeks by taking a shortcut. Does repayment necessarily require four weeks? No. What remains is a quality gap, and the system and its requirements may change. Estimate the current effort to close the relevant gap rather than equating it with the original time saving.
