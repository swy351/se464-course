1. Replicas with one shared trigger

A service has six replicas across three regions, but the same automation can change the networking configuration of every replica at once. Which statement best describes the remaining risk?

  1. Six replicas guarantee availability
  2. Three regions eliminate configuration risk
  3. A shared control mechanism can fail all replicas together
  4. Replication removes the need to test restoration

Worked answer

Best answer: C.

A shared control mechanism can create correlated failure across otherwise separate replicas.

Location diversity helps against some failures, but a global control action can cross those boundaries. Staged changes, isolation and independent recovery paths address different risks.

Check your work

  • Distinguishes replica count from failure independence
  • Identifies the shared control plane

2. Restore capacity or restore service?

A recovered service handles 160 jobs/s. New work arrives at 130 jobs/s, and 9,000 durable jobs are waiting. Under a deterministic fluid model with no retries or other bottleneck, estimate the drain time. What if recovered capacity is only 120 jobs/s?

Worked answer

At 160 jobs/s, spare capacity is 30 jobs/s; 9,000/30 = 300 seconds. At 120 jobs/s, the backlog grows by 10 jobs/s, so it cannot drain under these assumptions.

Durability preserves work but does not guarantee timely recovery. Real estimates also need retry behavior, service-time variation, downstream limits and an explicit priority policy.

Check your work

  • Computes spare capacity rather than total capacity
  • Obtains 300 seconds
  • Identifies instability at capacity 120 and states assumptions

3. Design for diagnosing the outage

A monitoring service uses its own ingestion pipeline to report all health signals. During an ingestion outage, operators lose dashboards. Propose two independent diagnostic or recovery mechanisms, and explain how to prioritize urgent alerts versus historical backfill.

Worked answer

Possible mechanisms include external synthetic probes with an independent alert path, and a separately reachable status/control service with retained diagnostic logs. Reserve capacity for urgent live signals and throttle historical backfill according to explicit data-loss and freshness requirements. Test the recovery path during a simulated ingestion failure.

Independence is a property of dependencies, not just a different dashboard name. A backfill policy should state what is delayed or discarded and why.

Check your work

  • Identifies independent dependencies
  • Connects prioritization to customer consequences
  • Includes a failure test and visibility during recovery
