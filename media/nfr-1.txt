Part 2 • Synthetic narration

Make quality measurable

Turn quality goals into measurable agreements. State the metric, workload, threshold and observation interval, including how failures are counted. Capacity needs an activity model, not just a user total. For other attributes, distinguish an outcome from a prescribed technology and test the outcome that stakeholders actually care about.

Optimize the system rather than isolated parts

Optimize the end-to-end flow, not isolated component rates. In this simplified producer-consumer model, throughput is limited by combined production or consumption, whichever is smaller. Balancing the stages can outperform equal allocations. State the equal-cost assumption and remember that full CPU utilization is still a capacity limit, with no practical headroom.
