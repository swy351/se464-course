State assumptions and show your reasoning. For design tasks, more than one solution may be valid; justify the tradeoffs.

1. Choose the correct acceptance evidence

A transit display must show a changed platform within five seconds for at least 99% of updates during a two-hour rush-hour test. The team reports an average update delay of 700 ms and no process crashes. Which additional evidence is essential to decide whether this requirement passed? Assume timestamps can be correlated from the accepted platform change to its appearance on the display.

  1. The median delay of successful updates only.
  2. The fraction of all eligible updates displayed within five seconds, counting missing updates as failures.
  3. The total number of servers that remained running.
  4. The average network round-trip time between two servers.
