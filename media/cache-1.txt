Proxies and cache hits

Follow a cache hit and a cache miss through a proxy. On a hit, saved data avoids backend work. On a miss, the backend supplies the value and the cache may retain it. Use a small LRU trace to make eviction concrete, then compare application-managed caching with a transparent intermediary.

Popularity, freshness and HTTP caching

Caching exploits repeated access, especially when popularity is uneven. The key design question is when reuse remains valid. Compare expiry, invalidation and versioned names, then apply HTTP cache directives. A successful cache reduces backend work while respecting freshness and identity boundaries; a high hit rate alone does not prove correctness.
